WHO WE ARE

Our story began in Radoviš with a passion for creating a place where self-care is more than just a routine—it’s an experience. With over 15 years of expertise in cosmetics and body care, we proudly offer high-quality treatments that blend tradition with modern trends, ensuring each client receives the best possible care. In 2023, we renovated our studio to provide an even more comfortable and luxurious atmosphere. With state-of-the-art equipment, advanced techniques, and a dedicated team, we guarantee top-tier treatments tailored to your needs. At Beauty Studio Chelsea, we believe beauty is about confidence and well-being. That’s why we focus on details, innovation, and personalized care to ensure every client leaves feeling refreshed, radiant, and satisfied. Your satisfaction is our greatest motivation!
